# Recorded sample sources

- Handpan: FreePats “Hang tuned in D minor”, recorded in July 2017 at the Medialab-Prado auditorium by Gonzalo and Roberto, performed by Mar. CC0 1.0. The website uses only D4, E4, F4, G4, A4, and B-flat4 notes produced by the original instrument, without pitch shifting.
- Acoustic drums: FreePats MuldjordKit, recorded by Lars Muldjord in 2010 and assembled for FreePats by Roberto. CC BY 4.0.
- Piano, acoustic guitar, flute, violin, and saxophone: tonejs-instruments sample library. Samples are CC BY 3.0. Its source notes identify VSO2 for piano, flute, and violin; the University of Iowa collection for acoustic guitar; and Karoryfer for saxophone.
- Ukulele: Sadiquecat’s “Ukulele” pack, recorded from a Miguel Almeria soprano ukulele in GCEA tuning with Zoom H5 XY microphones. The app uses 11 open-string and fretted-note recordings (sound IDs 726659, 726679, 726681, 726694, 726714, 726718, 726730, 726732, 726753, 726767, and 726787) to form real four-string chord voicings. CC0 1.0.
